### 1. 🚔 Police Harassment and Misconduct
**Category**: Protection  
**What it covers**:
- Police harassment is illegal
- No police can demand money (bribery is a crime)
- You have right to respectful treatment
- You can file complaints against police misconduct
- Compensation is available for harassment

**Key Points**:
- Police have authority but NO power to abuse citizens
- No officer can demand money
- Excessive force or threats are illegal
- You can file FIR against police
- Compensation available

**What to Do**:
1. Stay calm and polite
2. Get officer's name, badge, station
3. Document date, time, location, details
4. Take photos of injuries (if harassed physically)
5. File FIR against police misconduct
6. Get medical certificate if hurt
7. Write complaint to Police Commissioner
8. Consult lawyer about compensation

### 2. 👮 Your Rights During Police Arrest
**Category**: Protection  
**What it covers**:
- Police must tell you WHY they're arresting you
- You have RIGHT to remain silent
- You have RIGHT to lawyer - DEMAND IT
- Can't be detained more than 24 hours without court approval
- Can't be tortured or abused
- Family must be informed within 12 hours

**Key Points**:
- Police MUST tell reason for arrest
- Silence is your RIGHT and PROTECTION
- You CAN and MUST demand lawyer
- 24-hour detention limit (strict)
- No torture allowed
- Family notification required

**What to Do During Arrest**:
1. Ask: "Why am I being arrested?"
2. Say: "I want a lawyer" - repeat clearly
3. Do NOT answer questions without lawyer
4. Note officer names and badge numbers
5. Refuse to sign anything without lawyer
6. Demand medical exam before custody
7. Ask for water, food, bathroom access
8. Don't confess - your silence = protection

### 3. 📋 False FIR (First Information Report)
**Category**: Protection  
**What it covers**:
- False FIR is a serious CRIME itself
- You can file counter-FIR against the person
- You can claim compensation
- Police must investigate fairly
- Courts dismiss false cases with weak evidence

**Key Points**:
- Filing false FIR is crime under Section 182, IPC
- Counter-FIR is your strong legal remedy
- Compensation available for harassment
- Court can dismiss false allegations
- Biased investigation is illegal

**What to Do**:
1. Get copy of the FIR
2. Read allegations carefully
3. Gather evidence proving false
4. Get witness statements
5. File counter-FIR under Section 182 & 166A, IPC
6. Apply for anticipatory bail
7. Keep all communications safe
8. Work with lawyer on defense

## NEW STEP-BY-STEP GUIDES ADDED (2 new scenarios)

### GUIDE 1: 👮 When Police Scold/Harass You
**4 detailed steps with action items, cautions, and laws**

#### Step 1: During Police Interaction
What to do WHEN the police is scolding or harassing you:
- Stay calm and polite (don't argue)
- Memorize officer's name, badge, station
- Ask: "What is the reason for this treatment?"
- Don't accept threats, abuse, or money demands
- If threatened with false arrest, say: "I want a lawyer"
- Note any injuries and remember witnesses

**Cautions**:
- Avoid physical confrontation
- Stay respectful even if police aren't
- Any money demand is ILLEGAL - refuse firmly but calmly

#### Step 2: Document Everything
After the harassment:
- Write down exact date, time, location, what happened
- Get names and phone numbers of witnesses
- Take photos of injuries (if harmed)
- Get medical certificate from doctor
- Note police station name and officer details
- Keep copies of all communications

#### Step 3: File Complaint Against Police
Report the misconduct:
- File FIR at DIFFERENT police station
- Or file complaint with Police Commissioner
- Or file with State Police Accountability Authority
- Provide detailed account with date/time
- Submit all evidence
- Request investigation and action

**Related Laws**: Section 154 CrPC, Section 330 IPC, Article 21 Constitution

#### Step 4: Seek Legal Remedies
Legal action for compensation:
- Consult lawyer about civil suit
- File case under tort law
- Claim compensation for emotional distress
- Request court order for apology
- Get protection order if harassment continues

### GUIDE 2: 🔒 Your Rights During Police Arrest
**4 detailed steps with critical information for protection**

#### Step 1: Moment of Arrest - Know Your Rights
FIRST THING TO DO when arrested:
- Ask: "Why am I being arrested? Tell me clearly."
- Say: "I want to speak to a lawyer. I will not answer questions without a lawyer."
- Do NOT answer questions or make statements
- Do NOT sign any documents
- Remember officer name, badge, station
- Inform family or trusted person
- Ask for water, food, bathroom access

**Critical Cautions**:
- ANYTHING you say WILL be used against you
- Staying silent is NOT admission of guilt
- Your silence is your PROTECTION
- Police may make threats - this doesn't change your rights

#### Step 2: Police Custody (First 24 Hours)
What happens and what you can do:
- Police can hold you maximum 24 hours ONLY
- Must be taken to magistrate after 24 hours
- REFUSE to answer police questions (lawyer will advise)
- REFUSE to sign statements you don't understand
- Insist on medical exam before custody
- Demand to speak to lawyer (it's your Constitutional right)
- Ask for copies of all arrest documents

**Critical Point**: False confessions under pressure are INADMISSIBLE in court

#### Step 3: Appearance Before Magistrate
Within 24 hours, you'll be in court:
- Tell magistrate you want a lawyer
- Magistrate decides: release, bail, or jail
- Speak ONLY through your lawyer
- Tell if you were harmed or threatened
- Request bail immediately
- If bail denied, request judicial custody (safer than police)

**Why it matters**: Judicial custody is SAFER than police custody

#### Step 4: Bail and Judicial Custody
Getting released:
- Lawyer will argue for bail
- Provide: fixed address, family ties, job, residence proof
- Accept conditions: police station reporting, passport surrender
- If bail granted = released immediately
- If bail denied = go to jail (judicial custody) - still better than police custody
- From jail, lawyer can appeal for bail in higher court

**Important**: Some serious crimes have NO bail, but lawyer can still fight for it
