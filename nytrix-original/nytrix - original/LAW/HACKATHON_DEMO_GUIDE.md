## 💡 **COMMON DEMO QUESTIONS & ANSWERS**

**Q**: Is this real lawyer data?
**A**: This is mock data for hackathon demo. Real implementation would connect to actual lawyer database.

**Q**: Does this replace legal advice?
**A**: No - we provide awareness and guidance only. Always consult a qualified lawyer for your specific case.

**Q**: How is this free?
**A**: During hackathon, all features are free and open. Revenue model could include premium features or lawyer commissions.

**Q**: Why focus on men specifically?
**A**: Men often face unique legal challenges (false accusations, custody discrimination) that aren't widely addressed. We promote equality by addressing these gaps.

**Q**: How does the AI analyzer work?
**A**: Uses Google's Gemini API to analyze scenarios against our knowledge base of Indian laws and legal concepts.

**Q**: What if the laws change?
**A**: Our knowledge base would be updated regularly with new legal precedents and amendments.
