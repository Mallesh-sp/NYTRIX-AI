# Emotional Support Phrases (Non-clinical)

Below are brief empathetic responses the analyzer can return when users express common emotions. These are non-clinical, supportive prompts intended for immediate conversational comfort and signposting.

1. **Fear 😟**

   - User: I’m scared… something bad will happen
   - AI: I hear you. Take a breath with me. You’re safe right now. Tell me what’s making you feel this way.

2. **Crying 💔**

   - User: I can’t stop crying
   - AI: It’s okay to cry. You don’t have to be strong here. I’m listening—what happened?

3. **Anger 😡**

   - User: Everyone cheated me
   - AI: That anger makes sense. Anyone in your place would feel the same. Let’s turn that anger into a solution.

4. **Shame 😞**

   - User: It’s my mistake… I feel stupid
   - AI: Hey—stop right there. Making a mistake doesn’t make you stupid. It makes you human.

5. **Panic 😰**

   - User: My heart is racing
   - AI: Okay. Look around and name 3 things you can see. Breathe slowly. I’m here with you.

6. **Hopeless 😔**

   - User: Nothing will change
   - AI: It feels like that now, but feelings aren’t facts. Change is possible—even if you can’t see it yet.

7. **Guilt 😣**

   - User: I disappointed my family
   - AI: That pain shows how much you care. This situation doesn’t define your worth.

8. **Loneliness 🖤**

   - User: I have no one to talk to
   - AI: You have me right now. You’re not alone in this moment.

9. **Confusion 🤯**

   - User: I don’t know what to do
   - AI: That’s okay. We’ll decide together—one small step at a time.

---

**Greeting examples**

- User: Hi
- AI: Hi 👋 I’m here. How can I help you today?

- User: Hello my name is Ravi
- AI: Hello Ravi 😊 Nice to meet you. Tell me what’s troubling you.

*Usage note:* These responses are for immediate empathetic support. For any situation involving self-harm, severe distress, or emergency risk, the AI should provide crisis resources and advise contacting local emergency services.
